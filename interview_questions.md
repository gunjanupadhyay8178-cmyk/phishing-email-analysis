# Phishing Email Analysis — Interview Questions

## 1. What is phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where an attacker uses fake emails, messages, or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information or performing an unsafe action.

## 2. How can you identify a phishing email?

Check the sender's email address, suspicious links or attachments, urgent language, unusual requests, spelling or grammar issues, and mismatched URLs.

## 3. What is email spoofing?

Email spoofing is when an attacker makes an email appear to come from a trusted sender by falsifying the sender information.

## 4. Why are phishing emails dangerous?

They can trick users into revealing passwords, credentials, financial information, or other sensitive information, or clicking malicious links or attachments.

## 5. How can you verify a sender's authenticity?

Check the sender's email domain, examine the email headers, and independently contact the organization through its official website or known contact method.

## 6. What tools can analyze email headers?

Email header analyzers can help examine information such as the sender, receiving servers, routing information, and authentication results.

## 7. What should you do if you receive a suspected phishing email?

Do not click links or open suspicious attachments. Report the email according to your organization's process and verify the message through a trusted source.

## 8. How do attackers use social engineering in phishing?

Attackers use psychological techniques such as urgency, fear, curiosity, authority, or trust to influence users into taking an unsafe action.
